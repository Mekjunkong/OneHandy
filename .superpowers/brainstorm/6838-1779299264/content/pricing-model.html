A — Fixed Transparent Prices

Every service has a listed price upfront. Users see a checkout total and pay instantly via Stripe. Maximum trust — expats know exactly what they're paying before committing. Ideal for standard services (AC, plumbing, gardening).

Booking flow: Select → Date/Time → Details → Pay → Confirmed

B — Quote-Based

No upfront prices. Users describe their needs and OneHandy responds with a quote. More flexible for complex jobs — but adds friction and a waiting step. Risks losing expats who want instant confirmation.

Booking flow: Select → Describe Issue → Submit → OneHandy contacts you

C — Hybrid: Fixed + Quote by Service Type

Standard services (AC, plumbing, gardening) show fixed prices and book instantly. Complex/variable jobs (roof repair, electrical assessment) show "from" price and get a quote. Emergency callout has its own fixed rate. Best of both worlds.

Clear badge labels (FIXED / QUOTE) so users always know what to expect

My recommendation: C — transparent pricing where possible, quotes only for genuinely variable jobs.

OneHandy — Platform Scope

This is a large multi-system platform. Let's pick a starting phase so we can spec and build it properly — one focused piece at a time.

Proposed Build Phases
1

Marketing Site + Booking Flow

Public landing page, service pages, transparent pricing, and a full customer booking flow with Stripe placeholders. This is the customer-facing foundation — the first thing expats see and use.

Pages: Home · Services · Pricing · Book Now · Confirmation · Technician Apply

2

Admin Dashboard

Internal job management, technician assignment, booking tracker, and payment overview. Built after Phase 1 so operators can manage the bookings coming in.

Views: Jobs · Technicians · Customers · Payments · Settings

3

Technician & Customer Portals

Authenticated areas for technicians (job updates, earnings) and customers (booking history, photo updates, subscriptions). Layered on after the core is live.

Views: My Bookings · Job Queue · Photo Uploads · Subscription Manager

Recommendation

Start with Phase 1. It delivers a fully usable, deployable product — expats can find OneHandy, browse services, and book a job. Phases 2 and 3 build on top of real demand signals.
